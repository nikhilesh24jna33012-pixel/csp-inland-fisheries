Our progress

Eight steps from questionnaire to report, and where we are now.

Our progress

8 of 8 steps done (100%). Each step below shows where we are.

  1. Plan the survey

    Done

    We wrote questions on species, methods, income, costs and problems.

  2. Visit the prawn farm

    Done

    On 12 July 2026 we went to a prawn farm at Mypadu, Nellore district, and saw the aerated ponds. We also watched a harvest: prawns netted from the pond, sorted into crates, weighed and packed in ice.

  3. See a fish harvest

    Done

    On 23 June 2026 we watched carp from a pond being sorted into crates for market.

  4. Look at the feed store

    Done

    We checked the prawn feed and pond supplements the farm keeps in stock, to understand what feeding costs.

  5. Talk to the farmers

    Done

    We interviewed 10 fish farmers, prawn farmers and traders and recorded their answers.

  6. Organise the answers

    Done

    We sorted responses into tables and looked for patterns.

  7. Share what we learned

    Done

    We gave simple tips on water quality, feeding and government schemes.

  8. Write it up

    Done

    We prepared the final report and this website.
