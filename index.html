Inland fisheries

A community service study of inland fisheries in Mypadu, Nellore district: who farms fish in ponds and tanks, what they earn, and what holds them back.

Why a small pond matters

A pond behind a village can feed a family, pay school fees and supply the local market.

Inland fisheries cover fish farming and fishing in freshwater: ponds, tanks, canals, rivers and reservoirs. We spoke with fish farmers, fishermen and traders in Mypadu, Nellore district to see how this work really looks on the ground.

Fish never touch the sea here.Everything we studied was raised or caught in freshwater close to home.
